About me

A little about me

The person behind the code: what I do, what drives me, and how I spend my time away from the keyboard.

Jeremy Ockenden Software Engineer · London, UK

Hello!

I'm a software engineer and Computer Science graduate from the University of Bath, with a strong foundation in Python and a focus on robust backends and data pipelines.

I build and integrate RESTful APIs with FastAPI and Flask, and design databases with SQL and Supabase. My work has ranged from a 20-year stock market database and its daily ETL pipeline to a machine learning model that analyses satellite imagery. I enjoy work that takes messy, real-world data and turns it into something reliable and useful.

I'm a collaborative engineer and at home in Agile/Scrum teams, whether that's an 8-person squad at Publicis Sapient or working directly with a CEO as a freelancer. I also enjoy the other side of the job: gathering requirements, presenting ideas and explaining technical work to people who aren't engineers.
